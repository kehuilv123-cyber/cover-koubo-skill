# Two-Second Retention Script Standards

## Source

Adapted from the creator's original two-second-retention production SOP.

## Core Metric

Two-second bounce rate means the share of viewers who swipe away within the first 2 seconds. Lower is better because more viewers choose to continue watching, giving the video more chance to be recommended.

## Core Goal

In the first 2 seconds, the viewer should quickly feel:

- Interested.
- Able to understand the topic.
- Emotionally related.
- Willing to keep watching.
- Aware that the video has value.

Execution principle:

```text
前 2 秒必须让用户知道：这条视频和我有关。
```

## Topic Value Gate

Before writing the script, classify the topic into at least one value type:

| Type | Suitable content | Judgment standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 需求型 | Tutorial, guide, knowledge sharing | Does the viewer have a clear problem to solve? |
| 共鸣型 | Campus, life record, story, casual video | Will the viewer feel "this is me"? |
| 稀缺型 | Travel, experience, observation | Is this something the viewer rarely sees? |
| 热点型 | Most content types | Can it connect to platform trends, popular BGM, or hot topics? |

Avoid opening from a topic that is too niche. If the content is deep, start from a familiar everyday scene, then move into depth.

## First 2-Second Design

The first 2 seconds should contain only the most important elements:

- 1-2 strong visuals.
- One concise spoken line.
- One core subtitle line.
- One attention-grabbing sound, BGM, or sound effect.

Do not use slow setup.

Avoid openings like:

- "大家好，我是..."
- "今天给大家分享一下..."
- "最近很多朋友问我..."
- Long empty shots.
- No subtitle, no sound, no key image.

Preferred openings:

- Direct question.
- Direct conclusion.
- Highlight moment first.
- Conflict first.
- Rare image first.
- User pain point first.

## Opening Line Formulas

Use one of these formulas when writing the first spoken line:

| Type | Formula | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 提问型 | 你有没有发现... | 你有没有发现，大学里越努力的人反而越焦虑？ |
| 结果型 | 我发现了一个... | 我发现了一个大学生周末低成本旅行路线。 |
| 痛点型 | 很多人都忽略了... | 很多大学生不是不努力，而是选错了学习方式。 |
| 悬念型 | 我原本以为...直到... | 我原本以为这个景点很普通，直到我走到最后一段。 |
| 共鸣型 | 如果你也是... | 如果你也是普通大学生，这条视频你一定懂。 |

## Visual Execution

The first frame must:

- Put the key person or core object in the center visual area.
- Make the video point understandable at a glance.
- Prefer close-up, medium-close, or dynamic images.
- Avoid meaningless wide shots, empty scenes, or slow walking shots.
- Move a highlight moment to the front when useful.

For the user's account:

- Knowledge sharing: face + clear subtitle + scene pain point.
- Campus content: dorm, classroom, canteen, library, or other highly recognizable campus scene.
- Travel Vlog: destination highlight + budget, route, or experience result.

## Sound Execution

The beginning cannot feel dry or flat. Use:

- Clear spoken line.
- Rhythmic BGM.
- Transition sound.
- Emphasis sound effect.
- Platform-licensed or otherwise rights-cleared music when it fits.

Sound should quickly wake attention and make the viewer feel the video has entered the key point.

## Subtitle And Text Standard

Opening subtitle should be short, accurate, and sharp.

Highlight one of:

- Pain point.
- Result.
- Contrast.
- Number.
- Suspense.
- Keyword.

Examples:

- 大学生低成本旅行路线
- 别再这样学了
- 100 元玩一天实测
- 大学里最容易被忽略的能力

Use dynamic subtitles or emphasized text for keywords, but do not overload the screen.

## Mandatory Script Output

When generating a video script, include:

```text
选题价值判断：
前 2 秒目标：
第一帧画面：
第一句话：
核心字幕：
声音/BGM/音效：
A 版痛点开头：
B 版悬念开头：
C 版结果开头：
完整口播文案：
拍摄提示：
发布前检查：
```

## Pre-Publish Checklist

Before publishing, check:

1. Does the first 2 seconds directly enter the point?
2. Is the first frame attractive?
3. Is the first spoken line strong?
4. Can viewers instantly understand the topic?
5. Is there sound, BGM, or sound effect stimulation?
6. Do subtitles reinforce the core information?
7. Has meaningless setup been removed?
8. Does the topic have demand, resonance, scarcity, or trend value?

If two or more items fail, rewrite or re-edit the opening before publishing.

## Iteration Rule

For the same topic, test three openings:

- A version: pain point opening.
- B version: suspense opening.
- C version: result opening.

After publishing, compare two-second bounce rate, average watch time, completion rate, like rate, comment rate, save rate, and follower conversion. Reuse the lowest-bounce opening pattern.

Starter template patterns:

- 大学生一定要知道的...
- 我用一天时间实测了...
- 普通大学生最容易忽略的...
- 这个地方真的适合学生党吗？
- 如果你也在大学迷茫，先看这个...
