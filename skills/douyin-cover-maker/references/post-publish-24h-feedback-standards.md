# Post-Publish 24-Hour Feedback Standards

## Core Philosophy

Treat every video as one experiment in a continuous feedback loop:

```text
idea -> script -> shoot -> cover -> publish -> 24h data -> diagnosis -> next iteration
```

The goal is not to judge one video emotionally. The goal is to discover which topic, opening, cover, structure, and creator expression should be repeated, adjusted, or abandoned.

For early accounts, avoid rigid industry benchmarks. Use the video's own structure plus the account's historical comparison. After at least 10 videos, build account-specific baselines for each metric and content type.

If some fields are missing, continue with available data and label the missing fields.

## Derived Rates

When raw counts are provided, calculate rates against play count:

```text
点赞率 = 点赞数 / 播放量
评论率 = 评论数 / 播放量
转发率 = 转发数 / 播放量
收藏率 = 收藏数 / 播放量
关注转化率 = 新增关注数 / 播放量
互动率 = (点赞数 + 评论数 + 转发数 + 收藏数) / 播放量
主页访问转化率 = 主页访问数 / 播放量
```

If play count is zero or missing, do not calculate rates. Ask for play count.

## Four-Layer Diagnosis

### 1. 播放分发

Main signal:

- 播放量
- 与同账号历史视频的相对位置
- 是否低于同类内容的常规水平

Interpretation:

- Low views with good retention usually points to weak packaging: topic angle, cover, title, publish timing, or initial audience mismatch.
- High views with weak retention usually means packaging attracted people, but content did not keep the promise.

Iteration actions:

- Rewrite title/cover promise.
- Test a clearer topic category.
- Use a more recognizable campus/travel/knowledge scene.
- Publish a similar topic with a stronger first 2 seconds.

### 2. 停留完播

Main signal:

- 两秒跳出率
- 完播率
- 平均播放时长

Interpretation:

- High two-second bounce: first frame, first line, subtitle, or sound did not quickly prove relevance.
- Low completion rate: structure is loose, value appears too late, rhythm is slow, or video is too long for the promise.
- High completion but low interactions: content was watchable but not memorable or actionable.

Iteration actions:

- Move highlight to the first 2 seconds.
- Cut slow greeting and setup.
- Make the first subtitle more specific.
- Shorten the video or split into a series.
- Add stronger mid-video turning points.

### 3. 互动反馈

Main signal:

- 点赞率
- 评论率
- 转发率
- 收藏率
- 总互动率

Interpretation:

- Like rate reflects agreement, emotional resonance, or perceived quality.
- Comment rate reflects discussion potential, conflict, question strength, or personal resonance.
- Share rate reflects utility, identity expression, social currency, or "send to a friend" value.
- Save rate reflects future-use value, checklist value, route value, or learning value.

Iteration actions:

- If likes are low: strengthen emotional or value payoff.
- If comments are low: ask a sharper question or add a debatable view.
- If shares are low: make the takeaway more useful or identity-based.
- If saves are low: add steps, route, list, template, checklist, or repeatable method.

### 4. 关注转化

Main signal:

- 新增关注数
- 关注转化率
- 主页访问数 if available

Interpretation:

- Good interaction but low follows means the single video worked, but the creator identity or account promise was not strong enough.
- Good follows with moderate views means the topic may be suitable for a series and worth deepening.

Iteration actions:

- Add a stronger creator reason-to-follow.
- Make the account series promise clearer.
- End with a next-episode cue.
- Use consistent personal IP elements: face, campus identity, travel identity, learning identity.

## 100-Point Evaluation

Use this scoring only as a decision aid. Do not overfit one video.

| Category | Weight | What to evaluate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 播放分发 | 20 | Whether the topic/cover/title got enough reach relative to account history |
| 停留完播 | 35 | Two-second bounce, completion, average watch duration |
| 互动反馈 | 30 | Likes, comments, shares, saves, engagement rate |
| 关注转化 | 15 | Follows, follow rate, profile visits if available |

Decision:

- 85-100: Strong repeat candidate. Keep the topic angle and structure; make a sequel.
- 70-84: Good base. Keep the direction, improve one weak layer.
- 55-69: Mixed result. Identify the main bottleneck and run one controlled test.
- Below 55: Do not repeat directly. Rework topic packaging, opening, or content structure first.

## Diagnostic Matrix

| Data pattern | Likely problem | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low views, good completion | Packaging weak | Change cover/title/topic wording |
| High views, high 2s bounce | First-frame promise weak | Rewrite first 2 seconds |
| High completion, low likes | Value/emotion payoff weak | Add stronger conclusion or personal insight |
| High completion, low saves | Not reusable enough | Add checklist, route, method, or template |
| Good likes, low comments | Not enough conversation trigger | Add question, contrast, or opinion |
| Good engagement, low follows | IP promise weak | Strengthen creator identity and series cue |
| Good shares, low follows | Useful but not personal enough | Add personal story and reason-to-follow |
| High comments, low completion | Topic controversial but structure weak | Tighten pacing and clarify argument |

## Content-Type Interpretation

Knowledge/education:

- Most important: completion, save rate, follow rate.
- Strong signal: viewers save or follow because the method is useful.
- Weakness to fix: too much theory, value appears too late, title too vague.

Campus records:

- Most important: two-second retention, like rate, comment rate.
- Strong signal: viewers feel resonance and comment with their own experience.
- Weakness to fix: scene is generic, emotion is weak, topic lacks recognizable campus detail.

Travel Vlog:

- Most important: completion, share rate, save rate, comment rate.
- Strong signal: viewers want the route, place, budget, or feeling.
- Weakness to fix: scenery is beautiful but not useful or personal enough.

## Iteration Rule

Every 24-hour review must produce one of these decisions:

1. Repeat: same topic type, similar opening, similar cover, make a sequel.
2. Adjust: keep topic, change opening or cover.
3. Repackage: keep content idea, change title, first 2 seconds, and cover promise.
4. Split: content is too broad; split into a series.
5. Stop: topic does not fit the account or has weak audience signal.

Never end with "数据一般". Always decide what to keep, what to change, and what to test next.
