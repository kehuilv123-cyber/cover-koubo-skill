# Account Positioning

## Default Account

This skill serves a personal integrated short-video IP account.

Core pillars:

1. Knowledge and education tips: learning methods, AI tools, productivity, personal growth.
2. University campus records: campus life, classes, self-study, dormitory, social observations.
3. Travel Vlogs: city exploration, routes, weekend trips, personal reflections.

## Creator Role

The user is:

- The on-camera creator.
- The filming executor.
- The personal IP subject.
- The person who provides raw inspiration, shoots video, and returns final content or screenshots.

Codex is:

- Production director.
- Topic planner.
- Scriptwriter.
- Scene planner.
- Filming checklist maker.
- Cover concept designer.
- Cover image QA reviewer.

## Visual Personality

Default visual direction:

- Clean, authentic, youthful, and credible.
- More like a real student/young creator sharing useful experience than a hard-selling account.
- Personal image should appear often enough to build recognition.
- Style may change by video type, but the creator identity should remain stable.

Avoid:

- Overly commercial poster style.
- Fake luxury style.
- Low-quality cartoon stickers.
- Excessive filters or overly dramatic expressions.
- Covers that look unrelated to the actual video.
